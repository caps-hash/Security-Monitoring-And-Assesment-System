# SAMS user guide

Sign in at `/login` with the email and password issued for the account. Use the sidebar to move between pages. On a small screen, open the menu button. Sign out from the sidebar.

## HR

- Personnel: create an employee or supervisor, edit the profile and email, and activate or deactivate an account.
- Supervisors: review supervisor accounts.
- Duty assignment: save posts, locations, and shifts, then assign an employee to a supervisor, post, location, shift, and start date.
- Attendance: review daily, weekly, and monthly summaries. A missing scheduled record is a potential absence. Official absence is recorded as unavailable.
- Assessments: set intervals, synchronize schedules, review evaluations, enter the official score, and release it.
- Final decision: save Passed or Not Passed, then release it. It stays hidden until release.
- Reports: filter personnel, attendance, assessment, decision, overdue, supervisor activity, SMS, and top-performer reports. Print from the browser. Download CSV, Excel, or PDF from the report page.
- SMS notifications: review delivery logs and reminder settings. Provider acceptance is not confirmed delivery.
- Audit log: review successful privileged actions. It is empty until an audited action occurs.

Combined ranking is not calculated until HR saves and approves weights that total 100%.

## Supervisor

- Review only the personnel assigned to you.
- Open an assigned employee's profile, assignment, and attendance.
- Open the evaluation schedule, including upcoming and overdue items.
- Save a draft, upload a PDF, Word document, or image, and submit the evaluation.
- If HR returns the evaluation, correct it and submit it again.
- View a released result only where the existing assessment rules allow it.
- You cannot open another supervisor's personnel, change an official score, or change a final decision.

## Employee

- Review your profile, assigned post, duty location, duty shift, and start date.
- Change your password from Account settings after entering the current password.
- Record Time In and Time Out with the camera. Allow location access when the browser asks. The attendance page records the date and time.
- Review your own DTR history.
- Review your assessment schedule and status.
- A score, rating, feedback, or final decision appears only after HR releases it.

An inactive account cannot continue into the dashboard. Contact HR if the sign-in page says the account is inactive.
